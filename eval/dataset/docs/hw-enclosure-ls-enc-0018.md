# Hardware Note: Enclosure LS-ENC-0018

Synthetic document, Larkspur Systems wiki (fictional).

The LS-ENC-0018 is the weatherproof cabinet that holds the board, the power supply unit, the battery, the modem and the cable terminations. It mounts on a wall or on the mast base.

## Specifications

- Ingress rating: IP65.
- Material: powder coated aluminium, 3 mm wall.
- Internal size: 400 mm by 300 mm by 200 mm.
- Weight empty: 4.2 kg.
- Mounting: four M8 bolts on a 360 mm by 260 mm pattern.
- Colour: light grey, to limit solar heating.

## Door seal

The door has a closed-cell foam gasket. Check the gasket at each site visit. Replace it if it is cracked or flattened. A gasket lasts about 5 years in full sun.

## Cable glands

Six glands pass cables through the bottom plate: mains, battery temperature sensor, GPS antenna, RF coax, Ethernet and a spare. Tighten each gland nut to 2.5 newton-metres. Fill an unused gland with a sealing plug. Never leave a gland open.

## Vent plug

A membrane vent plug equalizes pressure and lets moisture out. Replace the vent plug every 2 years. A clogged plug traps moisture and fogs the door window. The part number is LS-VNT-0004.

## Heater

A 15 W heater and a thermostat keep the inside above freezing. The thermostat switches the heater on at 2 degrees Celsius and off at 8 degrees Celsius. The heater is the largest load at northern sites. See the power note for the effect on battery runtime.

A second thermostat cuts the heater at 45 degrees as a safety limit.

## Interior layout

Mount the board and modem on the upper plate. Put the battery on the floor of the cabinet, because it is the heaviest part and it likes the warmest air near the heater. Keep at least 30 mm of space around the power supply unit for airflow.

## Inspection checklist

1. Door seal clean and intact.
2. No water marks inside.
3. Desiccant pack blue, not pink.
4. Gland nuts tight.
5. Heater warm to the touch when the temperature is below 2 degrees.
6. Door latches work with gloves on.

## Grounding

Bond the cabinet to the site ground rod with 10 mm squared cable. Measure the ground resistance once a year. It should be under 10 ohms.
