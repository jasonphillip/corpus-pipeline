# Onboarding: Your First Week

Synthetic document, Larkspur Systems wiki (fictional).

This page is for new engineers on the platform, firmware and data teams. It lists what to do each day. Your manager will confirm the plan on day 1.

## Day 1

- Collect your laptop and security key from the office manager.
- Request access through the access form. The form needs your manager as approver. Requests for the `viewer` role are granted the same day. Requests for the `ops` role need a completed training record.
- Join the ops channel and the team channel.
- Meet your onboarding buddy. Each new engineer has a buddy from the same team for the first 3 months.

## Day 2

- Read the architecture overview, the glossary and the page on frame formats.
- Read the three most recent incident reports. They show how the fleet fails in practice.
- Set up the development environment. See the dev environment page.

## Day 3

- Visit the fleet dashboard and find your first three nodes. Look at their SNR history, GPS status and outbox size.
- Attend the daily standup at 14:30 UTC.

## Day 4

- Run the launch readiness check in observer mode with the on-duty engineer. You only watch.
- Pick a starter ticket from the label `good-first-ticket`. Starter tickets are small and have a named reviewer.

## Day 5

- Submit your first pull request.
- Write down three questions that the documentation did not answer. Send them to your buddy. We fix the docs from these questions.

## Weeks 2 to 6

- You shadow the on-call engineer for two rotations before you carry the pager. Each rotation lasts one week.
- Join one launch day from the ops side and one site visit if you work on hardware.

## Safety training

Anyone who goes to a site completes the mast safety course in the first month. The course takes half a day. It covers working at height, wind limits, lock-out of battery power and first aid for cold injuries.

## What we expect

- Ask questions in public channels so that others learn from the answers.
- Do not run commands on a production node that you do not understand.
- Write down what you learn. The wiki is the shared memory of the team.

## Contacts

Your onboarding buddy is the first point of contact. The platform lead is the second. For access problems, contact the security officer.
